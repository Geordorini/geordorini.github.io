Lumen — a toy language that compiles to WebAssembly

A small statically-typed language with a complete compiler written from scratch in JavaScript: a hand-written lexer, a recursive-descent parser, a type checker, and a WebAssembly binary emitter. Everything below runs in your browser — the bytes are assembled here and handed straight to WebAssembly.instantiate.
